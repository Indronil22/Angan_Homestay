Everything you need to know about listing your home and hosting travelers through HomeStay Gallery.

You can become a host by creating a host account and submitting your property details. You may be asked to provide information such as your property description, location, amenities and photographs.

You can list eligible homes, rooms, guesthouses, cottages or other suitable accommodation that you are legally permitted to offer to travelers.

Hosts may need to provide information including the property location, description, available amenities, photographs, house rules and other details required to create an accurate property listing.

Yes. You can upload photographs that accurately represent your property. Photos should be clear, relevant and legally authorized for use by you.

Hosts can update their property information when necessary. Keeping your listing accurate and up to date helps travelers understand what to expect from their stay.

HomeStay Gallery may provide communication features that allow travelers and hosts to discuss the property, availability and stay-related questions.

Yes. Hosts can communicate reasonable house rules and expectations that travelers should follow during their stay. These rules should be clearly communicated in the property listing.

Cancellation conditions may depend on the booking features available on HomeStay Gallery. Where supported, the applicable cancellation terms will be communicated to travelers before they confirm a stay.

If you cannot honor a confirmed arrangement, you should inform the traveler as soon as possible. Any refund or alternative arrangement will depend on the circumstances and the applicable booking terms.

Any applicable host fees, commissions or other charges will be communicated by HomeStay Gallery before they apply to your listing or booking.

Property listings may go through an approval process before being displayed publicly on HomeStay Gallery. A listing may require changes or may not be approved if the information does not meet HomeStay Gallery's requirements.

If you need assistance with your property or hosting experience, you can contact the HomeStay Gallery team through the Contact Us page.
