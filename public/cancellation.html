Cancellation
Policy

Understand how cancellations, changes and refunds may work when booking a stay through HomeStay Gallery.

1. Overview

HomeStay Gallery connects travelers with hosts offering homestay experiences. Cancellation terms may vary depending on the property, host and booking arrangement.

Before confirming a booking, travelers should carefully review the cancellation conditions associated with the specific property.

2. Property-Specific Cancellation Policies

Individual hosts may offer different cancellation conditions for their properties. The applicable cancellation policy will depend on the terms presented for the particular stay.

Travelers are responsible for understanding these conditions before confirming a booking.

3. Cancellation by a Traveler

If a traveler needs to cancel a booking, they should do so as soon as possible using the cancellation method provided by HomeStay Gallery or by contacting the host where appropriate.

Whether a traveler receives a full refund, partial refund or no refund will depend on the cancellation conditions applicable to that booking.

4. Cancellation by a Host

Hosts are expected to honor confirmed arrangements and provide the accommodation described in their listing.

If a host needs to cancel a confirmed stay, the traveler should be informed as soon as reasonably possible. Any refund or alternative arrangement will depend on the circumstances and the applicable booking terms.

5. Changes to a Booking

Requests to change booking dates, the number of guests or other booking details may be subject to host approval and availability.

A change to a booking may also affect the total price or applicable cancellation conditions.

6. Refunds

Where a refund is applicable, the amount and timing of the refund will depend on the cancellation terms associated with the booking and the payment method used.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution involved.

7. No-Show

If a traveler does not arrive for a confirmed stay without cancelling the booking, the booking may be treated as a no-show.

Any refund for a no-show will depend on the cancellation policy applicable to the property.

8. Exceptional Circumstances

In certain exceptional circumstances, cancellation or changes may need to be handled differently. Examples may include serious emergencies, natural disasters, government restrictions or other events outside a user's reasonable control.

Any special consideration will depend on the circumstances and the policies applicable at the time.

9. Communication With Hosts

Travelers are encouraged to communicate with their hosts as soon as they know that they may need to cancel or change a stay.

However, communicating with a host does not automatically change the official cancellation conditions associated with a booking.

10. Disputes and Support

If a traveler and host cannot resolve a cancellation or refund issue, they may contact HomeStay Gallery for assistance.

HomeStay Gallery may review the circumstances and information available to us in order to assist with resolving the issue.

11. Changes to This Policy

HomeStay Gallery may update this Cancellation Policy as the platform, booking system and services develop.

Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about a cancellation or booking, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
