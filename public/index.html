ABOUT ANGAN

Stay with people, not just places.

Angan is a community-driven homestay platform created for travelers who want more than just a room for the night. We believe the best way to experience a destination is through the people, stories and homes that make it special.

Instead of staying in places that feel the same everywhere, Angan helps you discover unique homes hosted by people who know their destinations best. From peaceful mountain homes and family-run stays to homes tucked away in places you may never find through traditional travel platforms, every stay offers a chance to experience somewhere differently.

For travelers, Angan makes it easier to discover authentic stays and connect with local hosts. For hosts, it creates an opportunity to open their homes, share their stories and welcome travelers from around the world.

We are building Angan around a simple idea — travel should feel personal. Because sometimes the most memorable part of a journey isn't the destination itself, but the people you meet and the home where you stayed.

HOW IT WORKS

From discovering a home to experiencing it.

Angan makes finding and staying in a homestay simple, personal and meaningful. Discover a place, connect with the person behind it and make your stay your own.

01

Explore

Discover unique homestays in destinations you want to explore. Browse homes, view photos, learn about the property and find a stay that feels right for you.

02

Meet Your Host

Get to know the person behind your stay. Learn about their home, their story and what makes their place special before you decide to stay.

03

Connect & Plan

Have a conversation with your host, ask questions and discuss your requirements. Plan your stay directly with someone who knows the destination.

04

Stay & Experience

Arrive as a guest and experience the destination from a local perspective. Stay in a real home, discover something new and leave with a story to tell.
