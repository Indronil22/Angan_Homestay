Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Angan collects, uses and protects information when you use our platform.

1. Introduction

Angan ("Angan", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit or use the Angan platform.

By using Angan, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Angan, we may collect information that you provide directly to us.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, account information and other details that you choose to provide when creating an account or communicating with us.

If you create a host account, we may also collect information about your property, including its location, description, photographs, amenities and other information necessary to create and manage a property listing.

3. Information About Your Use of Angan

We may collect information about how you interact with our website, such as the pages you visit, features you use and general technical information about your device and browser.

This information helps us understand how Angan is being used and allows us to improve the platform and user experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to provide, maintain and improve Angan and its features.

This may include using information to create and manage accounts, display property listings, facilitate communication between travelers and hosts, respond to support requests, maintain platform security and improve our services.

5. Information Shared With Other Users

Some information may be visible to other users when you use certain features of Angan.

For example, information included in a host's public property listing may be visible to travelers so they can understand the property and the experience being offered.

We encourage users to avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information publicly through their profiles, property listings or other areas of the platform.

6. Communication Between Travelers and Hosts

Angan may provide features that allow travelers and hosts to communicate with each other.

Information shared during these communications should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for arranging or discussing a stay.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Angan may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality, remember preferences and understand how visitors use the platform.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some functionality of the website.

9. Third-Party Services

Angan may use third-party services to support certain website functions, such as hosting, analytics, communication, payments or other technical services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the security of the platform.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to the personal information we hold about you. These may include requesting access to, correction of or deletion of certain information.

If you would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact us through our Contact Us page.

Angan is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law.
