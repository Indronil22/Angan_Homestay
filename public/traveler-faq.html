Everything you need to know about discovering homestays and staying with Angan hosts.

Angan is a homestay platform that connects travelers directly with local hosts. Instead of staying in standardized hotels, Angan helps you discover real homes, meet local people and experience destinations from a more personal perspective.

Explore the available homestays on Angan, choose your preferred destination and browse the properties available there. You can view property details, photos and information about the host before deciding where you would like to stay.

Yes. Angan is designed around connecting travelers with hosts. You can communicate with your host, ask questions about the home and understand the stay before making your final decision.

Properties submitted by hosts may go through an approval process before being displayed publicly on Angan. We are building Angan with the goal of creating a trustworthy experience for both travelers and hosts.

Where available, travelers can request a live tour from the host. This allows you to see the property and ask questions before deciding whether it is the right stay for you.

The booking process will depend on the property and the features available on Angan. You can connect with the host and discuss availability and stay details before confirming your plans.

Cancellation terms may vary depending on the property and the host's cancellation policy. Always review the cancellation conditions associated with your stay before confirming your booking.

If you need help with a booking, property or any other issue, you can contact the Angan support team through the Contact Us page.
